# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-10

Hurrair VIP Trading provides trading signal access and VIP verification.

## Information We Collect

- Email address submitted for VIP verification.
- Signal usage status needed to show Free or VIP access.
- Device notification token after Firebase push notifications are enabled.

## Information We Do Not Collect

- Exness password.
- Trading account password.
- Payment card details.

## How We Use Information

- To verify whether a user should receive VIP access.
- To show the correct Free or VIP signal feed.
- To send signal notifications after push notifications are enabled.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. Admins may manually compare submitted emails with the Exness partner dashboard to verify VIP eligibility.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner/admin.
